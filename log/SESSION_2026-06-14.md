### Diagnosis (systematic-debugging skill)
- **Symptom 1 root cause:** the local MinIO podman container's clock had drifted ~18h 41m behind the host. The S3 SDK rejected `bucket_exists` with `RequestTimeTooSkewed`. Verified by `curl -I localhost:9000/...` returning `Date: Sat, 13 Jun 2026 23:00:47 GMT` while host was `Sun, 14 Jun 2026 17:41:39 GMT`. The MinIO clock has since resynced on its own; the error no longer reproduces.
- **Symptom 2 root cause:** `bronze_eu_orchestrator` (`src/tcg_platform/defs/eu_pipeline_orchestrator.py:17-19`) called `execute_in_process` **sequentially** despite a log message saying "in parallel" and the 2026-06-02 spec requiring parallelism. When DE's Zyte fetches were slow, UK + exchange_rates sat idle and the UI looked frozen. The spec-vs-code drift is the same kind of bug that was caught in PR #17 (2026-06-11 tcg-raw layer); the parallel orchestrator step was just never wired.
